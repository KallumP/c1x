# Level sources

The sources that [custom levels](../doc/custom_levels.md) are built from in the level editor, one folder each: a map modelled in Blender, as its `.obj`, `.mtl` and images, a `level.cfg` with its `turn`, `scale`, sky (`sky_top`, `sky_bottom` and `sky_split`) and camera (`camera_distance`, 0.5 to 3 times its usual distance from Crash, and `camera_angle`, 10 to 80 degrees above him, 42 usual) if they aren't the defaults, and an `objects.json` placing objects in it, if it has any (see [Objects](#objects)). The editor writes `level.cfg` and `objects.json`. The built level goes to `build/levels/<name>`.

Some level sources are included in the repository, made for c1x, listed below. Open one in the level editor, then click **Build** and **Boot** (or press F5). The rest of `level_src/` is ignored by git, so sources you add stay local.

| Source | Origin |
|---|---|
| `tide_ruins/` | Made for c1x, with procedural textures. |
| `mossy_creek/` | Made for c1x, with procedural textures: a more organic map, its shores and ledges curves rather than straight lines. |
| `sunset_canyon/` | Made for c1x in Blender (its `.blend` is included), with procedural textures: a long, winding desert canyon at sunset, about twice mossy creek's length, with boxes laid out as in the game's own levels. |
| `acid_cave/` | The acid cave cut from the original game (its files are in `streams/s0000004`), exported with `npm run export-cut-level` (in `tools/level_editor`) through Blender (its `.blend` is included): its own models, textures and lighting, its boxes, the acid killing Crash, and fixed pads in place of its platforms over the acid. Past where the cut level ends, it goes on, reimagined in its style by `scripts/blender/extend_acid_cave.py`: a drop into a crystal geode, a fungal grotto of giant mushrooms over an acid marsh, and a river gorge crossed on a rock arch and boulders, then a collapsed temple swallowed by the rock (a tiki hall between acid channels, a crystal cavern of stepping stones, and acid falls down to a round end chamber). |

## Objects

An object in `objects.json` has a `kind`:

- `spawn`: where Crash starts (one at most)
- `end`: the warp pad that ends the level (one at most)
- `box`: a box, by its `type` (see [Boxes](#boxes)); `stack` makes a stack of them
- `enemy`: an enemy, by its `type` (see [Enemies](#enemies))
- `obstacle`: a part of the level with a mind of its own, by its `type` (see [Obstacles](#obstacles)): something Crash rides, like a lily pad, or has to get past

Each object also has `at`, where it is in Blender. The [level editor](../doc/custom_levels.md#2-place-objects-in-the-level-editor) writes all of these, and the settings below, for you.

### Settings many objects share

What each enemy and obstacle takes is listed in its entry below; this is what those settings mean. Distances in the entries are at scale 400 (a level's `scale` in `level.cfg`), so they grow and shrink with it.

- **Where it comes from.** Custom levels are built from Jungle Rollers, which has every kind of box, and its plant, rolling stone and badgers. Every other enemy and obstacle is taken from a level of the original game, named in its entry, so that level's files (`streams/s00000xx.nsd` and `.nsf`) have to be in `streams/`. Many also take room for more texture pages, of the 16 the game holds at once: the build says when a level has too many.
- **`raise`**: how far above `at` it is, in metres. Objects that ride or float are raised over the floor; the editor starts some already raised.
- **`path`**: the nodes it moves through, `"path": [[x, y, z], ...]`, 2 to 20 of them, each in metres from where it stands (`at`, raised by `raise`), so the path moves with it. Enemies walk it there and back from its first node (those that come out of the ground, like badgers, run it once, first to last); obstacles go along it and back, easing at each end. Without one, it has the default path its entry gives. Its points are spaced as its own level's, so it moves at its own pace however far apart the nodes are. Things on paths go straight through walls and off edges (except where an entry says it flies), so keep paths on clear floor: the level editor warns about one that isn't. A bat roost, leader bats and a lab assistant have one node instead, where their bats fly to or their vials land.
- **`speed`**: for an enemy on a path, how many times the game's own pace it walks at, 0.25 to 3 (1 by default), the same all along its path; its legs don't move any faster, so a fast one looks like it's sliding. For an obstacle on a path, how fast it goes in metres a second, 0.25 to 6, whatever its path's length (its default is in its entry).
- **`every`** and **`offset`**: for an obstacle on a timer, the seconds between its pushes, flames, slams or turns (2 to 20; its default is in its entry), and the seconds they're put off by (0 to 20, 0 by default). Without an offset every one of a kind goes at once: give a row of them offsets of 0, 2 and 4 so they go one after another, or two of them 0 and 3 so they take turns.
- **`reverse`**: `"reverse": true` sends it the other way: round, across or back, as its entry says.
- **`count`** and **`delay`**: for enemies that come out in numbers, how many come out at once (1 to 6), and for bats, the seconds between flocks (1 to 20).

### Enemies

- `plant` (Jungle Rollers): a biting plant that bites Crash if he walks into it, and stays where it is. Spun, it's flung straight back, away from the camera, and breaks the boxes it passes within about 3 m of where it stood; boxes further back are safe.
- `snake` (Temple Ruins; one texture page): the cobra, hidden at `at` until Crash comes within a few metres, when it rises and strikes at him. In Temple Ruins it rises out of a pot, which is part of that level's map, so here it comes up out of the floor; model a pot round its spot if you want one. Put it right beside the route, where Crash won't see it coming.
- `gunman` (Cortex Power; one texture page): a gangster standing at `at` with a pistol in each hand. Once Crash comes within `range` metres (2 to 40, 16 by default) and isn't more than about 5 m past him, he shoots every second or so; a shot that hits kills Crash, and shots stop at walls. Spin him and he's knocked flying. Put him where Crash has to get to him across open ground, or somewhere he can't reach.
- `blue_river_plant`, `green_river_plant` (Up the Creek; three texture pages shared with its fish, lily pads and leaves): biting plants, low and wide with red jaws, their leaves blue or green. Each stays at `at`, rearing up and snapping now and then; walk into its jaws and it swallows Crash. It can't be spun or jumped on, so go round it.
- `crab` (N. Sanity Beach): walks back and forth along its `path` (default: 4 m with its middle at `at`, across the map like the rolling stone). It always faces the camera, walking sideways, forwards or backwards. Crash dies if it walks into him; spun, it's flung away.
- `turtle` (N. Sanity Beach; its textures shared with the crab): walks back and forth along its `path` (default: 5 m with its middle at `at`, along the route, the way the camera faces), turning to face the way it walks. Jumping on it bounces Crash off and turns it onto its back, where he can stand on it; spun, it's knocked away.
- `native` (The Great Gate; no textures): a native with a shield, standing guard on its `path` (default: about 5 m with its middle at `at`, across the map), starting at its first node. When Crash comes within about 6 m it walks along its path to stay in front of him, shield first, and goes back to its first node when he leaves. Walking into its shield pushes Crash back along its path, jumping on it bounces him off, and a spin from the front does nothing: get round it and spin it from the side or behind. Put its path across Crash's way, or along it, so it can block him.
- `badger` (Jungle Rollers): badgers (skunks) running along their `path` once and diving into the ground at its end (default: 13 m with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn, so they run at Crash). When Crash comes within about 8 m of its first node, `count` of them (2 by default) pop out there one after another, turning towards him; while he stays near, more follow. Crash dies if one runs into him; spun, it's knocked away.
- `warthog` (Road to Nowhere): waits at its `path`'s first node until Crash comes within about 10 m, then charges back and forth along it, edging up to about 2 m sideways towards him (default path: 13 m with its middle at `at`, along the route). Its sideways charge is across the map, so keep its path along the route, as Road to Nowhere's are. Crash dies if it runs into him, and a spin doesn't stop it: jump over it.
- `spider` (Fumbling in the Dark): hangs on its thread about 4 m above `at`, drops to the floor there, climbs back up and drops again, turning slowly as it hangs. Crash dies if he walks into it; spun, it's knocked away. Put it over the route, where Crash has to time his way under it.
- `fish` (Up the Creek): leaps out of the floor at `at` as if out of a river, in a high arc about 5 m up to land 4 m across the map, then leaps back, splashing each time; `reverse` makes it leap the other way across. A spin knocks it away. It needs no water under it. Put it where Crash has to time his way past its leap.
- `lab_assistant` (Stormy Ascent; no textures): stands at `at` facing its one node, where its vials land (default: about 4.7 m across the map, the other way from a rolling stone), and every 4 seconds throws a vial there, which bursts into flame. The vial lands on its node over ground at the spot's height; higher or lower ground in the way stops it sooner or later. Walking into the assistant kills Crash (a burst right by him didn't, in testing); spun, it's knocked away. Stand it beside the route so its vials land across Crash's way.
- `walking_spider` (Fumbling in the Dark): works like badgers, running along its `path` once and going down out of sight at its end (default: 8 m with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn). When Crash comes near its first node, `count` of them (1 by default) come out one after another; while he stays near, more follow. Crash dies if one runs into him; spun, it's knocked away.
- `bat` (code from Sunset Vista; two texture pages for bats of either kind): bats from a roost at `at`, raised by `raise` (the roost itself can't be seen). While Crash is still more than about 3 m short of it, it sends out `count` bats (4 by default) one after another, and another flock every `delay` seconds after (10 by default, counted from when the last flock set off). They swoop down about 3 m and fly to the roost's node (default: 25 m along the route back towards the spawn, at Crash), wobbling a little, then go; their swoop keeps its dip wherever the node is, but the roost still waits for Crash along the route. Crash dies if one flies into him; spun, it's knocked away. Raise the roost about 5 m over the floor Crash will be on (the editor starts a new one that high) so the bats come down to his height, and give them a long clear run: they fly through walls.
- `leader_bats` (Sunset Vista): two leader bats, the first hovering at `at`, raised by `raise`, the second at its node (default: 15 m back across the map, the other way from a rolling stone). Every `delay` seconds (4.5 by default), wherever Crash is, a flock of `count` bats (5 by default) dives from high over the first leader, flies past it to the second and climbs away. Crash dies if a bat flies into him; spun, one is knocked away. Spin both leaders and the flocks stop: the bats still out drop fruit, and no more come. Raise the first leader about 1.5 m over the floor Crash will be on (the editor starts a new one that high), and put the second at about his height too, across his way.
- `monkey` (The Great Gate; no textures): waits at an end of its `path` until Crash comes near, then scurries along it on all fours to the other end and stands up taunting him (default: 6 m with its middle at `at`, across the map), starting at its first node. Crash dies if he walks into it.
- `lizard` (The Lost City; one texture page, shared by its lizards): the green lizard, leaping back and forth along its `path` in high arcs, from node to node (default: 8 m with its middle at `at`, across the map, its points 4 m apart). Its `speed` sets how far apart the points it leaps between are, so a fast one goes further each time.
- `red_lizard` (The Lost City): hops along its `path` towards whichever point is nearest Crash, following him as he goes (default: 12 m with its middle at `at`, across the map, its points 2 m apart; its `speed` spaces them as a lizard's). Crash dies if either lizard lands on him or he walks into one; a spin pushes him back off it unless he has Aku Aku's masks, which knock it away.
- `walking_lizard` (The Lost City): the spiky lizard on all fours, pacing back and forth along its `path`, turning to walk the way it goes (default: 2 m with its middle at `at`, across the map). Crash dies if he walks into it; jump on it to kill it (a spin pushes him back off it unless he has Aku Aku's masks).
- `robot_dog` (Heavy Machinery; one texture page): a grey robot dog walking back and forth along its `path`, turning to face the way it goes (default: 4 m with its middle at `at`, along the route). Crash dies if it walks into him; it stops when he stands in its way. It can't be spun or jumped on (Crash bounces off its back), so jump over it.
- `barrel_roller` (Toxic Waste; one texture page): a fat man in a suit, standing at the first node of his `path` and rolling a barrel down it every couple of seconds (default: 12 m with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn, so he stands 6 m beyond `at`). Crash dies if a barrel or the man runs into him; jump the barrels. Give him a path down the route at Crash.
- `camera_robot` (Castle Machinery; one texture page, the gunman's too): a spiked hovering robot with a periscope camera on top, floating back and forth along its `path`, bobbing and puffing smoke (default: 3 m straight up from `at`, as Castle Machinery's rise and fall in its shafts). Crash dies if he touches it, spinning or not, and it can't be jumped on: keep away from it. It flies, so its path can go anywhere; it's only drawn red if it runs through a wall.
- `bird` (Slippery Climb; one texture page): a big brown vulture flapping back and forth along its `path`, a lap every 4 seconds (default: 3 m straight up from `at`, as Slippery Climb's swoop beside its climb). Crash dies if he flies or walks into it (its wings reach about 2 m); jump on it or spin it to knock it down. It flies, as the camera robot does.
- `electric_scientist` (The Lab; one texture page): a walking lab assistant with sparks crackling at his hands, walking along his `path` to the end nearest Crash and waiting there (default: 12 m with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn). Crash dies if he gets within about a metre of him, jumping too; spin him to knock him back along his path, though he walks back. He can share a level with the lab assistant and the robot dog.
- `blob` (The Lab; one texture page): yellow slime blobs coming out of the floor at the first node of their `path` and hopping along it, one at a time, sinking into the floor at its last (default: 13 m with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn). A blob comes out once Crash is 8 to 14 m from the first node and hasn't reached it, so give it a path down the route at him. Crash dies if one hops into him; jump on it to squash it (a spin only knocks him aside).
- `flying_camera` (Heavy Machinery, as the robot dog; one texture page, the robot dog's too): a grey ball with a lens out front and a blue light on top, floating back and forth along its `path`, turning its lens to Crash (default: 3 m straight up from `at`; Castle Machinery's are mostly 16 m). Its spot is its middle. Crash dies if he walks or spins into it; jumping on it bounces him off. It flies, as the camera robot does.

### Obstacles

Each one's name in the level editor's palette is in brackets.

- `lily_pad` (Up the Creek; three texture pages shared with its river plants and fish): four round leaves lying flat at `at`. With Crash on it, it sinks about 1 m, rises back up and sinks again, carrying him. Raise it over a gap or a drop: at the bottom of its sink it's 1 m below where it's placed, so on the floor it sinks into it. It needs no water under it.
- `leaf` (Up the Creek, as the lily pad): a floating leaf drifting along its `path` and back every few seconds, swaying from side to side (default: Up the Creek's, about 6 m back towards the spawn). Its path takes as long as Up the Creek's leaf's, so a longer one makes a faster drift; every leaf drifts in step with the others. Crash rides it if he lands on it from above; on the floor it passes through him, so raise it over a gap.
- `wooden_peg` ("Wood platform (spinnable)"; The Great Gate; three texture pages): a platform from the totem, standing folded up at `at` until Crash spins it, then flipping out as a step about 2.8 m out towards the camera, which he can stand on, folding back up about 8 seconds later and dropping him. It needs no wall behind it. Raise it to where Crash should step up to, and put a few in a row up a wall or a drop for him to climb.
- `rolling_stone` (Jungle Rollers): a stone wheel that comes with its stone track, set on the floor at `at`, rolling back and forth along it across the map (at right angles to the way the spawn's arrow points). The track is about 14 m long and its middle, where the stone dips lowest, stands a box's height over the floor at `at`, so Crash has to jump up into it. Put it somewhere flat and wide enough. `"start"` is how far along its track it starts, from 0 (its first end, the default) to 1 (its far end); `reverse` makes it roll back towards its first end first.
- `mossy_slide` (The Great Gate): the slide from its mossy planks, which carries Crash down a slope while he stands on it. It's invisible: only its slide is placed, so model the slope itself in Blender, where the level editor draws it. It starts at `at` (its top end) and runs `"length"` metres (2 to 20, 4 by default) down at `"angle"` degrees (5 to 30, 14 by default), across the map turned `"rotation"` degrees anticlockwise seen from above (0 to 359, 0 by default: 90 is the way the spawn's arrow points, 180 back across). It slides Crash on any floor within about 0.8 m of that line, so keep other floors away from it.
- `rising_spiked_log` ("Spiked log (rising)"; The Great Gate, with the wood platform's texture pages): waits as a spiked stub in the floor at `at`, then pushes up out of it about 4 m and back down, on a timer (`every` 6 by default). It kills Crash if he touches it, even as a stub.
- `falling_spiked_log` ("Spiked log (falling)"; as the rising one): hangs about 4 m over `at` and comes down onto the floor there, then goes back up (`every` 6 by default). Place it on the floor it should come down on, and keep the space above it clear. It kills Crash if he's under it.
- `fire_pit` (The Great Gate, with the wood platform's texture pages): a red bowl on the floor at `at`, reaching about 2 m out towards the camera, which shoots up a column of flame (`every` 4 by default). The flame sets Crash alight and he burns to ash; a level with one also takes Crash's burning from The Great Gate.
- `dropping_pillar` ("Pillar (drops)"; Boulder Dash; two texture pages): a mossy stone pillar, its top at `at` and reaching about 6 m down. It stands still until Crash stands on it, then sinks away about 4 seconds later, dropping him, and doesn't come back. Raise it out of water or a pit as a stepping stone he has to keep moving across.
- `sliding_pillar` ("Pillar (slides)"; as the dropping one): the same pillar, sliding along its `path` and back the whole time (default: about 2 m across the map), at its `speed` (1 m/s by default).
- `tall_thin_gate` ("Gate (tall, thin)"; Boulder Dash; one texture page): a wooden gate standing on the floor at `at`, a post about 4 m tall with two planks across it, about 2.3 m wide. Crash knocks it over by walking into it.
- `low_wide_gate` ("Gate (low, wide)"; as the tall one): a row of log stumps about 1 m tall and 6 m wide, which Crash can stand on, or knock over by walking into it.
- `pushing_block` ("Block (pushes out)"; Sunset Vista; two texture pages): a big carved stone block about 4 m tall, pushing out about 5 m from `at` towards the camera and back again, shoving Crash before it (`every` 2 by default).
- `pushing_platform` ("Platform (pushes out)"; Sunset Vista; three texture pages, with the block's): a stone platform, its top just under `at` (raise it to where Crash should stand), sliding out about 4.5 m towards the camera and back in, to stand on while it's out (`every` about 2.3 by default).
- `rotating_pots`, `rotating_platforms` ("Pots (rotating)", "Platforms (rotating)"; Sunset Vista): stone pots, or flat stone platforms, going round an upright circle across the map like a big wheel, its middle at `at`, for Crash to ride (`every` 6 by default a turn; `reverse` sends them round the other way). `"platforms"` is how many go round (1 to 7: 3 pots or 2 platforms by default) and `"radius"` how far out, in metres (1 to 10, 3.5 by default).
- `dropping_platform` ("Platform (drops when stood on)"; Sunset Vista): a stone platform sticking out 1 m towards the camera from `at`, as if from a wall. When Crash stands on it, it shakes for `"shake"` seconds (0.25 to 5, 2/3 by default), drops for `"down"` seconds (0.5 to 10, 2 by default), then comes back.
- `seesaw` ("Seesaw"; Sunset Vista): a row of five stone blocks, its middle one's top at `at`, the others 0.8 m apart either side (about 4 m end to end), tipping by where Crash stands on them.
- `slamming_walls` ("Walls (slamming)"; Temple Ruins): a carved stone wall, its two halves (each about 3 m across and 2.6 m tall) standing either side of `at` and slamming shut there, then opening again (`every` 3 by default). They crush Crash if he's caught between them. Put them across his way, so he runs through while they're open.
- `rising_pillar` ("Pillar (rises)"; Temple Ruins; one texture page): a stone pillar, its top at `at` its lowest, rising about 4 m and sinking back, over and over, for Crash to ride up to a ledge (`every`, the seconds a rise and fall takes, 4 by default). Put `at` level with the floor and it rises out of it.
- `carrying_platform` ("Platform (carries Crash)"; Temple Ruins): a small stone platform, its top at `at`, standing still until Crash stands on it, then carrying him along its `path` and back (default: 6 m straight across the map), at its `speed` (1.5 m/s by default: Temple Ruins' own pace). Keep its path clear of walls: it carries him through them.
- `rotating_pegs` ("Pegs (rotating)"; Temple Ruins): stone columns going round a level circle about `at`, their tops raised by `raise`, carrying Crash round on them (`every` 20 by default a turn; `reverse` sends them the other way round). `"radius"` is how far out (1 to 10 m, 4 by default, to the nearest 400 game units), and `"platforms"` how many: 4, 5, 8 or 9 (5 by default; others go to the nearest of those, as the game has no more).
- `bounce_pad` ("Bounce pad"; Castle Machinery): a spring standing on `at`, bouncing Crash up off it whenever he lands on it.
- `steam_vent` ("Steam vent"; Castle Machinery): puffs steam up from `at` that burns Crash (`every` 3 by default). It has no pipe of its own (Castle Machinery's are part of its map), so put it on something that looks like one.
- `moving_platform` ("Platform (moves along a path)"; Slippery Climb): a mossy stone slab, its top at `at`, sliding along its `path` and back the whole time (default: 4 m straight across the map), at its `speed` (2 m/s by default).
- `lift_platform` ("Platform (lifts up and down)"; Slippery Climb): a stone slab, its top at `at`, going along its `path` and back the whole time (default: 3 m straight up), at its `speed` (2 m/s by default).
- `falling_platform` ("Platform (falls when stood on)"; The Lost City): a carved stone slab about 1 m square, its top at `at`, falling as soon as Crash stands on it, then floating back up.
- `timed_plank` ("Plank (breaks after a moment)"; Road to Nowhere): a wooden plank 1.2 m across, its top at `at`, dipping under Crash, then breaking and falling `"delay"` seconds (1 to 20, 3 by default) after he first gets onto its middle (near its ends, it only dips).
- `breaking_plank` ("Plank (breaks at once)"; Road to Nowhere): a cracked plank the same size, breaking and falling as soon as Crash stands on it.
