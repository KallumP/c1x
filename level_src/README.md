# Level sources

The sources that [custom levels](../doc/custom_levels.md) are built from in the level editor, one folder each: a map modelled in Blender, as its `.obj`, `.mtl` and images, a `level.cfg` with its `turn`, `scale`, sky (`sky_top`, `sky_bottom` and `sky_split`) and camera (`camera_distance`, 0.5 to 3 times its usual distance from Crash, and `camera_angle`, 10 to 80 degrees above him, 42 usual) if they aren't the defaults, and an `objects.json` placing objects in it, if it has any (see [Objects](#objects)). The editor writes `level.cfg` and `objects.json`. The built level goes to `build/levels/<name>`.

Some level sources are included in the repository, made for c1x, listed below. Open one in the level editor, then click **Build** and **Boot** (or press F5). The rest of `level_src/` is ignored by git, so sources you add stay local.

| Source | Origin |
|---|---|
| `tide_ruins/` | Made for c1x, with procedural textures. |
| `mossy_creek/` | Made for c1x, with procedural textures: a more organic map, its shores and ledges curves rather than straight lines. |
| `sunset_canyon/` | Made for c1x in Blender (its `.blend` is included), with procedural textures: a long, winding desert canyon at sunset, about twice mossy creek's length, with boxes laid out as in the game's own levels. |
| `acid_cave/` | The acid cave cut from the original game (its files are in `streams/s0000004`), exported with `npm run export-cut-level` (in `tools/level_editor`) through Blender (its `.blend` is included): its own models, textures and lighting, its boxes, the acid killing Crash, and fixed pads in place of its platforms over the acid. Past where the cut level ends, it goes on, reimagined in its style by `scripts/blender/extend_acid_cave.py`: a drop into a crystal geode, a fungal grotto of giant mushrooms over an acid marsh, and a river gorge crossed on a rock arch and boulders, then a collapsed temple swallowed by the rock (a tiki hall between acid channels, a crystal cavern of stepping stones, and acid falls down to a round end chamber). |

## Objects

An object in `objects.json` has a `kind`:

- `spawn`: where Crash starts (one at most)
- `end`: the warp pad that ends the level (one at most)
- `box`: a box, by its `type` (see [Boxes](#boxes)); `stack` makes a stack of them
- `enemy`: an enemy, by its `type` (see [Enemies](#enemies))
- `obstacle`: a part of the level with a mind of its own, by its `type` (see [Obstacles](#obstacles)): something Crash rides, like a lily pad, or has to get past

Each object also has `at`, where it is in Blender, and can have `raise`, how far above that it is. The [level editor](../doc/custom_levels.md#2-place-objects-in-the-level-editor) writes all of these for you. Custom levels are built from Jungle Rollers, which has every kind of box, and its plant, rolling stone and badger. Other enemies are taken from the level they're in, such as the crab from N. Sanity Beach, so the game's files for that level have to be in `streams/` too.

### Enemies

- `plant`: a biting plant that bites Crash if he walks into it, and stays where it is. Spun, it's flung straight back, away from the camera, and breaks the boxes it passes within about 3 m of where it stood (at scale 400); boxes further back are safe.
- `snake`: Temple Ruins' cobra, hidden at `at` until Crash comes within a few metres (at scale 400), when it rises and strikes at him. In Temple Ruins it rises out of a pot, which is part of that level's map, so here it comes up out of the floor; model a pot round its spot if you want one. Put it right beside the route, where Crash won't see it coming. It comes from Temple Ruins, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and it takes room for one more texture page.
- `gunman`: Cortex Power's gangster, who stands at `at` with a pistol in each hand and, once Crash comes within `range` metres of him (2 to 40, 16 by default) and isn't more than about 5 m past him, shoots at him every second or so; a shot that hits him kills him, and his shots stop at walls. Spin him and he's knocked flying. Put him where Crash has to get to him across open ground, or somewhere he can't reach. He comes from Cortex Power, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and he takes room for one more texture page.
- `blue_river_plant`, `green_river_plant`: Up the Creek's biting plants, low and wide with red jaws, their leaves blue or green. Each stays at `at`, rearing up and snapping now and then; walk into its jaws and it swallows Crash. It can't be spun or jumped on, so go round it. Up the Creek's river plants and fish come together, so a level with any of them takes room for three more texture pages (of the 16 the game holds), however many it has.
- `crab`: N. Sanity Beach's crab, which walks back and forth along its `path`: unless you give it one, a straight line 4 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, across the map like the rolling stone (see [Obstacles](#obstacles)). It always faces the camera, walking sideways, forwards or backwards. Crash dies if it walks into him; spun, it's flung away.
- `turtle`: N. Sanity Beach's turtle, which walks back and forth along its `path`: unless you give it one, a straight line 5 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, along the route (the way the camera faces). It turns to face the way it walks. Jumping on it bounces Crash off and turns it onto its back, where he can stand on it; spun, it's knocked away. It shares its textures with the crab, so a level with both takes no more room for textures than one with crabs.
- `native`: The Great Gate's native with a shield, which stands guard on its `path`: unless you give it one, a straight line about 5 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, across the map like the crab. It starts at its path's first node. When Crash comes within about 6 m it walks along its path to stay in front of him, shield first, and when he leaves it goes back to its first node. Walking into its shield pushes Crash back along its path, whichever way that runs, and jumping on it bounces him off the shield it holds up; a spin from the front does nothing. Get round it and spin it from the side or behind. Its model has no textures, so it takes no room for them. Put its path across Crash's way, like The Great Gate's natives, or along it, so it can block him.
- `badger`: Jungle Rollers' badgers (skunks), which run along their `path` once, from its first node to its last, and dive into the ground there: unless you give them one, a straight line 13 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn, so they run at Crash. When Crash comes within about 8 m of the path's first node, `count` of them (1 to 6, 2 by default) pop out of the ground there, one after the other, and run along it, turning towards him; while he stays near, more follow. Crash dies if one runs into him; spun, it's knocked away. They're Jungle Rollers' own, so they take no room for textures.

- `warthog`: Road to Nowhere's warthog, which waits at its `path`'s first node until Crash comes within about 10 m, then charges back and forth along it, edging sideways towards him (up to about 2 m off its path). Unless you give it one, its path is a straight line 13 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, along the route (the way the camera faces). Its sideways charge is across the map, so keep its path along the route, as Road to Nowhere's are. Crash dies if it runs into him, and a spin doesn't stop it: jump over it.

- `spider`: Fumbling in the Dark's spider, which hangs on its thread about 4 m above `at` (at scale 400), drops to the floor there, climbs back up, and drops again, over and over, turning slowly as it hangs. Crash dies if he walks into it; spun, it's knocked away. Put it over the route, where Crash has to time his way under it.
- `fish`: Up the Creek's fish, which leaps out of the floor at `at` as if out of a river, in a high arc (about 5 m up) to land 4 m across the map (at scale 400), then leaps back, over and over, splashing each time. `"reverse": true` makes it leap the other way across. A spin knocks it away. It needs no water under it. Put it where Crash has to time his way past its leap.
- `lab_assistant`: Stormy Ascent's lab assistant, who stands at `at` facing its `path`, one node `[[x, y, z]]` in metres from it where its vials land (unless you give it one, about 4.7 m at scale 400 across the map, the other way from a rolling stone), and every 4 seconds throws a vial there, which bursts into flame where it lands. It lands on the spot over ground at the spot's height; ground higher or lower in the way stops it sooner or later (a burst right by Crash didn't hurt him in testing; walking into the assistant does). Spun, the assistant is knocked away. Stand it beside the route so its vials land across Crash's way. It comes from Stormy Ascent, so its files need to be in `streams/`; its models have no textures, so it takes no room for them.
- `walking_spider`: Fumbling in the Dark's walking spiders, which work like badgers: they run along their `path` once, from its first node to its last, and go down out of sight there. Unless you give them one, their path is a straight line 8 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn, so they run at Crash. When he comes near the path's first node, `count` of them (1 to 6, 1 by default) come out there, one after the other; while he stays near, more follow. Crash dies if one runs into him; spun, it's knocked away. Both spiders come from Fumbling in the Dark, so its files need to be in `streams/`.

- `bat`: Temple Ruins' bats, from a roost at `at`, raised by `raise` (the roost itself can't be seen). Once it's in play, while Crash is still more than about 3 m short of it, it sends out `count` bats (1 to 6, 4 by default), one after the other, and another flock every `delay` seconds after (1 to 20, 10 by default, counted from when the last flock set off). They swoop down about 3 m and fly to the roost's `path`, one node `[[x, y, z]]` in metres from the roost (unless you give it one, 25 m at scale 400 along the route back towards the spawn, at Crash), wobbling a little, then go. Their swoop keeps its dip wherever its end is; turned across the map, the roost still waits for Crash along the route. Crash dies if one flies into him; spun, it's knocked away. The swoop dips about 3.3 m below the roost, so raise it about 5 m over the floor Crash will be on (the editor starts a new one that high) for the bats to come down to his height, and give them a long clear run; they fly through walls. Their code comes from Sunset Vista, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and bats of either kind take room for two more texture pages (of the 16 the game holds).
- `leader_bats`: Sunset Vista's bats, with two leader bats: the first hovers at `at`, raised by `raise`, and the second at its `path`, one node `[[x, y, z]]` in metres from the first (15 m back across the map at scale 400, the other way from a rolling stone, unless you give it one). Every `delay` seconds (1 to 20, 4.5 by default), wherever Crash is, a flock of `count` bats (1 to 6, 5 by default) dives down from high over the first leader, flies past it to the second, a little behind them, and climbs away past it. Crash dies if a bat flies into him; spun, one is knocked away. Spin both leaders and the flocks stop: the bats still out drop fruit, and no more come. Raise the first leader about 1.5 m over the floor Crash will be on (the editor starts a new one that high), and put the second at about his height too, for him to spin them and the bats to fly at him. Put them across Crash's way.

- `monkey`: The Great Gate's monkey, which waits at an end of its `path` until Crash comes near, then scurries along it on all fours to the other end and stands up there taunting him. Unless you give it one, its path is a straight line 6 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, across the map like the crab. It starts at its path's first node. Crash dies if he walks into it. Its model has no textures, so it takes no room for them.
- `lizard`: The Lost City's green lizard, which leaps back and forth along its `path` in high arcs, from node to node. Unless you give it one, its path is a straight line 8 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, across the map like the crab, its points 4 m apart.
- `red_lizard`: The Lost City's red lizard, which hops along its `path` towards whichever point is nearest Crash, following him as he goes. Unless you give it one, its path is a straight line 12 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, across the map, its points 2 m apart. Crash dies if either lizard lands on him or he walks into one; a spin pushes him back off it unless he has Aku Aku's masks, which knock it away. Both come from The Lost City, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and they take room for one more texture page.
- `walking_lizard`: The Lost City's spiky lizard on all fours, which paces back and forth along its `path`, turning to walk the way it goes. Unless you give it one, its path is a straight line 2 m long (at scale 400) with its middle at `at`, across the map. Crash dies if he walks into it; jump on it to kill it (a spin pushes him back off it unless he has Aku Aku's masks). It comes from The Lost City, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and takes room for one more texture page.
- `robot_dog`: Heavy Machinery's grey robot dog, which walks back and forth along its `path` (unless you give it one, 4 m long at scale 400 with its middle at `at`, along the route like the turtle's), turning to face the way it goes. Crash dies if it walks into him; it stops when he stands in its way. It can't be spun or jumped on (Crash bounces off its back), so jump over it. It comes from Heavy Machinery, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and it takes room for one more texture page.
- `barrel_roller`: Toxic Waste's fat man in a suit, who stands at the first node of his `path` and every couple of seconds rolls a barrel down it (unless you give it one, 12 m long at scale 400 with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn, so he stands 6 m beyond `at`). Crash dies if a barrel or the man runs into him; jump the barrels. Give him a path down the route at Crash. He comes from Toxic Waste, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and takes room for one more texture page.
- `camera_robot`: Castle Machinery's spiked hovering robot with a periscope camera on top, which floats back and forth along its `path`, bobbing and puffing smoke (unless you give it one, its path goes straight up 3 m at scale 400 from `at`, as Castle Machinery's rise and fall in its shafts). Crash dies if he touches it, spinning or not, and it can't be jumped on: keep away from it. Its path can go anywhere, in the air too; it's only drawn red if it runs through a wall. It comes from Castle Machinery, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and takes room for one more texture page (the gunman's too).
- `bird`: Slippery Climb's big brown vulture, which flaps back and forth along its `path`, a lap every 4 seconds (unless you give it one, its path goes straight up 3 m at scale 400 from `at`, as Slippery Climb's swoop up and down beside its climb). Crash dies if he flies or walks into it (its wings reach about 2 m); jump on it or spin it to knock it down. Like the camera robot's, its path can go through the air. It comes from Slippery Climb, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and takes room for one more texture page.
- `electric_scientist`: The Lab's walking lab assistant, electric sparks crackling at his hands, who walks along his `path` to the end nearest Crash and waits there (unless you give it one, his path is 12 m long at scale 400 with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn). Crash dies if he gets within about a metre of him, jumping too; spin him to knock him back along his path, though he walks back. He comes from The Lab, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and takes room for one more texture page. He can share a level with the lab assistant and the robot dog.
- `blob`: The Lab's yellow slime blobs, which come out of the floor at the first node of their `path` and hop along it, one at a time, sinking into the floor at its last node (unless you give it one, 13 m long at scale 400 with its middle at `at`, along the route back towards the spawn). A blob comes out once Crash is 8 to 14 m from the first node and hasn't reached it, so give it a path down the route at him. Crash dies if one hops into him; jump on it to squash it (a spin only knocks him aside). They come from The Lab, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and take room for one more texture page.
- `flying_camera`: Castle Machinery's (and Heavy Machinery's) grey flying camera, a ball with a lens out front and a blue light on top, which floats back and forth along its `path`, turning its lens to Crash (unless you give it one, its path goes straight up 3 m at scale 400 from `at`; theirs are mostly 16 m). Its spot is its middle. Crash dies if he walks or spins into it; jumping on it bounces him off. Its path can go anywhere, in the air too; it's only drawn red if it runs through a wall. It comes from Heavy Machinery, as the robot dog does, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and takes room for one more texture page (the robot dog's too).

A crab's, turtle's, native's, warthog's, monkey's or lizard's `path` is the nodes it walks through, in order, there and back, starting at the first (badgers and walking spiders run it once, first to last): `"path": [[x, y, z], ...]`, 2 to 20 of them, each in metres from where the enemy stands (`at`, raised by `raise`), so the path moves with it. In the game its points are spaced as its own level's are, which keeps it walking at its own speed, however far apart the nodes are. It walks straight through walls and off edges, so keep its path on clear floor; the level editor warns about a path that isn't.

A crab's, turtle's, native's, badger's, warthog's, walking spider's, monkey's or lizard's `speed` is how many times the game's own pace it walks at, 0.25 to 3 (1 by default). It's the same all along its path, wherever the nodes are. Its legs don't move any faster, so a fast one looks like it's sliding. A lizard's speed sets how far apart the points it leaps or hops between are, so a fast one goes further each time.

### Obstacles

The lily pad and leaf are ridden, and come from Up the Creek, with its river plants and fish (its files need to be in `streams/`), taking the same three texture pages as them, however many of each a level has. Neither needs water under it.

- `lily_pad`: Up the Creek's lily pad, four round leaves lying flat at `at`. With Crash on it, it sinks about 1 m (at scale 400), rises back up and sinks again, carrying him with it. Raise it over a gap or a drop: at the bottom of its sink it's 1 m below where it's placed, so on the floor it sinks into it.
- `leaf`: Up the Creek's floating leaf, drifting from `at` about 6 m back towards the spawn and swaying from side to side, then back, every few seconds. Crash can ride it if he lands on it from above; on the floor it passes through him, so raise it over a gap. Its `path` is the nodes it drifts through, there and back: `"path": [[x, y, z], ...]`, 2 to 20 of them, each in metres from `at` (raised by `raise`). Without one it drifts Up the Creek's way. It drifts its path in as many steps as Up the Creek's leaf, easing in and out at the ends, so a longer path should make for a faster drift. It goes through walls, so keep its path clear of them. Every leaf drifts in step with the others.
- `wooden_peg`: a wood platform from the totem in The Great Gate ("Wood platform (spinnable)" in the level editor), standing folded up at `at` until Crash spins it. Then it flips out as a step towards the camera (about 2.8 m out at scale 400), which he can stand on, and folds back up about 8 seconds later, dropping him. It needs no wall behind it. Raise it to where Crash should step up to, and put a few in a row up a wall or a drop for him to climb. It comes from The Great Gate, so its files need to be in `streams/`, and it takes room for three more texture pages.
- `rolling_stone`: a stone wheel that comes with Jungle Rollers' stone track, set on the floor at `at`, and rolls back and forth along it, across the map: at right angles to the way the spawn's arrow points. The track is about 14 m long (at scale 400), and its middle, where the stone dips lowest, stands a box's height over the floor at `at`, so Crash has to jump up into it. Put it somewhere flat and wide enough. `"start"` is how far along its track it starts, from 0 (its first end, the default) to 1 (its far end), and `"reverse": true` makes it roll back towards its first end first, rather than on.
- `mossy_slide`: the slide from the mossy planks in The Great Gate, which carries Crash down a slope while he stands on it. It's invisible: only its slide is placed, so model the slope itself in Blender, as part of your map, where the level editor draws it. It starts at `at` (raised by `raise`, its top end) and runs `length` metres (2 to 20, 4 by default) across the map, down at `angle` degrees (5 to 30, 14 by default), at right angles to the way the spawn's arrow points, or back the other way with `"reverse": true`. It slides Crash on any floor within about 0.8 m of that line (at scale 400), so keep other floors there away from it. It comes from The Great Gate, so its files need to be in `streams/`.
